Settings

Choose a year and control how net19 prepares pages.

Loading

Preparation runs automatically, and popular sites are prepared in the background ahead of time. Cached sites skip the archive lookup. When a page no longer matches its archived layout, the year's fonts, colors, links and controls are applied instead.

Site access

net19 starts automatically on public websites. Use the toolbar button to pause a site. Chrome's site-access controls can restrict where the extension runs.

The site's homepage address is sent to the Internet Archive. Visited paths, searches, page contents, and cookies are not sent by net19. A fixed list of popular homepages is also looked up in the background; that list is the same for everyone and reveals nothing about you.

Only captures from the selected year are saved. Older fallback captures are used for the current visit only. Storage is limited to 100 snapshots and 4 MB.

Privacy

Archive rendering and layout matching happen locally. There is no net19 account, server, analytics, or remote AI. Archived scripts never run. A small number of archived PNG branding images may be decoded and saved with their snapshot.

Page styles and controls are matched by structure; pages that were redesigned receive the archived fonts, colors and controls by role. The extension does not reproduce arbitrary archived applications or execute their code.
