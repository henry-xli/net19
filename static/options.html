01

Your experience

Saved styles are reused locally. A slow archive can prepare the next visit, but it will never restyle a page after the loading gate closes.

02

Site access

Start with a few favorite sites using the net19 toolbar button, or enable it across the public web.

On enabled sites, net19 sends the site's homepage address to the Internet Archive to find historical styling. Your visited paths, searches, page contents, and cookies are never sent by net19.

04 / YOUR PRIVACY

A local habit. Not a tracking habit.

No net19 account, backend, analytics, or remote AI. Archive analysis happens in your browser. Archived scripts, fonts, and images never run on your live page.

Net19 adapts historical styling to modern sites; it does not recreate an old layout. Missing archives, unsupported designs, private addresses, and the current-year setting keep the current website. Availability and loading speed depend on the Internet Archive.
